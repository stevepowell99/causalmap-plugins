# Method notes

Notes on evaluation methods, written for a peer who already knows the field. Read the pages that bear on the question before writing the workflow; nobody needs all of them.

They are shared with Rubicon's hosted service, so some pages, the ones whose names end in "in Rubicon" above all, talk about Ruby, her subagents, the record, `plan.md` or tools named `mcp__rubicon`. None of those exist here. Read those passages as advice on how to design the workflow of pieces you write, not as tools you have. The same holds wherever a page mentions a group step, a `practice`, `check` or `review` setting, a second coder or adjudicator, or a skill by name: those belong to the hosted service. Here you group the labels yourself and you are the only coder, so do yourself what such a passage says the step is for.

- `contribution-analysis`: Contribution analysis. Setting out the theory of change a programme rests on, gathering the evidence for and against each of its links, taking the rival explanations seriously, and assembling what survives into a contribution story.
