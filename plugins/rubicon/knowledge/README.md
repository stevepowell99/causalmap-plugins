# Method notes

Notes on evaluation methods, written for a peer who already knows the field. Read the pages that bear on the question before writing the workflow; nobody needs all of them. Where the question names no method, or names one that may not fit, start with choosing-a-method.md; where it asks for a verdict of worth, read evaluative-rubrics.md.

Pages that describe a workflow, the ones whose names end in "in Rubicon" above all, do so in the pieces of Rubicon's open format: sample, code, group, tabulate, judge and write. Here you group the labels yourself and you are the only coder, so where a page speaks of grouping, do it yourself and record each row's kind as a nominal column.

One line per page: its name, its title and its first sentence.
