# Realist evaluation

Realist evaluation asks what works, for whom, in what circumstances, in what respects, and how. It refuses the question of whether a programme works. No programme works uniformly, and the variation is the finding. Pawson and Tilley set it out in *Realistic Evaluation* (1997).

Subsidiary pages: [CMO configurations](realist-evaluation-cmo.md), [testing and refining](realist-evaluation-testing.md), [in Rubicon](realist-evaluation-in-rubicon.md).

## What it establishes

A realist evaluation produces refined programme theory: a set of statements saying that in these circumstances, this resource triggers this reasoning in these people, producing this outcome, and in those circumstances it does not. Each statement is a context-mechanism-outcome configuration, and the set of them is what the evaluation delivers.

The claim rests on generative causation. A programme does not cause an outcome the way a force moves an object. It introduces resources into a setting. People reason about those resources in light of what they already have and already believe, and the outcome follows from that reasoning. So the causal power sits in people rather than in the intervention. That is why the same programme produces different results in different places, and why asking whether it works is the wrong question.

The ambition reaches past the case. Pawson insists a realist evaluation should be testing middle-range theory, meaning a theory abstract enough to travel between programmes and concrete enough to be wrong. A set of local configurations that explain this programme and nothing else has done half the work.

What it does not establish: an effect size, a verdict, or a recommendation about whether to continue funding. Those can be built on top of what it produces, and they are not what it produces.

## When it fits, and when it does not

It fits where the same intervention has produced different results in different places, and somebody wants to know why. It fits multi-site programmes, and anything where "it depends" is already the best available answer and nobody can say what it depends on. It fits where the reasoning of participants can actually be reached, which usually means interviews conducted for this purpose.

The second half.

**A commissioner who wants a verdict.** Realist evaluation answers a different question from the one most terms of reference ask. It will not produce a yes or a no. Where the reframe is refused, the work gets judged against a question it declined by design to answer. Settle that before starting, in writing.

**No variation to explain.** Realist evaluation feeds on differences between sites, groups and periods. One site, one group, one uniform result leaves nothing to configure. Process tracing is the better instrument for how a single case came out as it did. Where every site reports the same result, also ask whether the outcome measure is too coarse to show the variation that exists.

**No access to reasoning.** This is the constraint that bites hardest in practice. Documents record decisions and activities. They rarely record why somebody responded to an offer the way they did, and monitoring data records outcomes without reasoning. So a realist evaluation over documents and administrative data alone produces configurations whose mechanisms are guesses dressed as findings. What that material can support is the initial programme theory, which is real work and the first round of several, followed by a statement of the interviews the rest would need.

**Interviews not conducted realistically.** Realist interviewing puts the emerging theory to the respondent and asks them to correct it, which is a different conversation from asking about their experience. Material gathered conventionally supports some realist analysis and rarely reaches the mechanism, because nobody was ever asked the question that surfaces reasoning. Retrofitting is common. It mostly produces plausible mechanisms nobody has tested.

**One pass, on a short timeline.** The method is a cycle: articulate the theory, test it, refine it, test again. A single round of data collection against an initial theory is the first quarter of it. Where the budget allows one round, say what that can produce, which is candidate configurations rather than tested ones.

**A mechanism that is not about people reasoning.** Fixing a supply chain has a mechanism in the ordinary engineering sense, and realist evaluation's mechanism is a human response to a resource. Where the causal story really does run through logistics rather than through anybody's reasoning, the framework fits badly and something simpler will do.

**Somebody asking which context factor matters most.** Realist configurations are packages, and asking which element matters most dissolves the configuration.

## What data it needs

Interviews with participants and with implementers, conducted so that the theory is put to them. The [testing page](realist-evaluation-testing.md) covers the technique, which matters more here than the sample size.

Outcome data across sites or groups, good enough to show a pattern. You need something that varies before you can ask why it varies. The pattern does not have to be statistically respectable. A demi-regularity, in Lawson's term as realist evaluators use it, is a semi-predictable tendency rather than an effect.

Contextual material about each site: what was already there, who ran it, what the local rules and relationships were. This is what most evaluations record thinnest and what realist analysis needs most.

Existing theory and literature, because the middle-range theory being tested should connect to something somebody has already argued. Start from the programme's own logic model and nothing else, and the configurations cannot travel.

## The steps in practice

1. Elicit the initial programme theory, from documents, from implementers, and from relevant literature.
2. Express it as candidate CMO configurations, each of which could be wrong.
3. Identify the outcome pattern across sites or groups, and find the demi-regularities worth explaining.
4. Design data collection to test the configurations, including who to interview and what to put to them.
5. Interview realistically, refining the theory as you go.
6. Analyse: for each configuration, what supports it, what refutes it, what refines it.
7. Rewrite the configurations. Record what changed and why.
8. Test the rewritten set, ideally on new material or a held-back site.
9. Abstract towards middle-range theory: what does this say beyond this programme?
10. Report the configurations that survived, the ones that did not, and the route between.

Steps 1, 4, 5 and 8 are done with people (implementers, participants, a held-back site), and a workflow over documents cannot take them: it drafts candidate configurations from the documents and reads what earlier interviews produced where they are in the corpus, and a Rubicon run can stop once the configurations are written and a few documents coded, for implementers to correct them, and again once the answer is drafted. Step 7 is the one this method is built around and the one most reports omit. A realist evaluation presenting its final configurations without the ones they replaced has hidden its own evidence.

## Who does what

The evaluator elicits and articulates the theory, decides which configurations are worth testing, conducts the realist interviews, and judges what the material does to each configuration. The programme's implementers are informants about the theory rather than only about the delivery. Their disagreement with a configuration is data.

Participants are informants about their own reasoning, which is the part only they have. Treating them as sources of experience rather than as theorists of their own response is the commonest way realist interviewing collapses back into ordinary qualitative work.

## Traps

Each comes with a test. The first two account for most published realist evaluation. Both are diagnosable from a single configuration read on its own.

- **The mechanism is a programme activity.** Training, mentoring, a helpline. Test: could the programme have done this without anybody thinking anything? If yes, that is a resource, and the mechanism is the reasoning it triggered.
- **The context is a setting.** Rural, urban, large, small, Malawi. Test: can you say how that feature enables or blocks the specific reasoning in the mechanism? Where you cannot, it is background.
- **The configuration cannot be false.** "In supportive contexts, supportive mechanisms produce positive outcomes." Test: write down what you would have to observe for it to be wrong. If nothing would do, it is not a hypothesis.
- **Realist as a label on ordinary qualitative work.** Thematic analysis with C, M and O headings added afterwards. Test: was there a theory before the data? Did it change? A theory that never moved was not being tested.
- **Configurations generated after the data and reported as tested.** Test: is there a dated initial set? Does the report show what changed between it and the final one?
- **Retroduction claimed, description delivered.** Test: were several candidate explanations generated for the pattern? Was anything written down that would distinguish them, before looking? One candidate that fitted is not retroduction.
- **Conventional interviews presented as realist.** Test: did anybody put a proposition to a respondent and invite them to correct it? Where nobody did, respondents could not refute a configuration, so the material can fail to support and cannot refute.
- **Epicycles.** Each contrary case answered by adding a context condition. Test: was the new condition specified before the next round? Does it exclude anything? A condition that only ever accommodates is a rescue rather than a refinement.
- **Too many configurations, none tested.** Fourteen in the report, each with two supporting quotes. Test: for each, what evidence would have refuted it? Was that evidence sought?
- **Outcomes reported only where intended.** Test: does any configuration name an outcome the programme did not want? A mechanism that fires produces effects in several respects. Reporting one of them is reporting the part somebody hoped for.
- **C, M and O treated as separable variables.** Cross-tabulating contexts against outcomes and calling the residue a mechanism. Test: does the configuration read as one causal statement, or as three lists? If the three could be recombined freely, the configuration is a table rather than a hypothesis.

## What good looks like

The mechanisms are recognisably about reasoning rather than about programme activities. The contexts are features that plausibly switch a mechanism on or off, rather than site names or demographics. Each configuration is specific enough to be wrong. The outcome pattern that prompted the analysis is shown. The initial configurations appear alongside the final ones, with an account of what moved and on what evidence. And a paragraph says what the findings imply beyond this programme, connected to theory somebody else could use.

A realist evaluation that can be assessed contains: the initial programme theory and where it came from; the initial configurations with their date; the outcome pattern and the demi-regularity being explained; the data collection design and how it was aimed at the configurations; the interview approach, said plainly enough that a reader can tell whether it was realist; for each configuration, the evidence for and against and each version with what prompted the change; the configurations dropped and why; the final set; and the middle-range theory the set instantiates.

A realist evaluation whose configurations only appear in their final form has published its conclusions and withheld its method.

## Combining it with other methods

Realist synthesis is the review sibling, aggregating across studies to build and test the same kind of theory. The RAMESES standards cover both: RAMESES I for synthesis, RAMESES II for evaluation, and both are worth reading as reporting checklists whatever else you do.

Process tracing works inside one configuration. Where a mechanism is contested and one site holds good documentary evidence, tracing it there tests what the configuration asserts.
