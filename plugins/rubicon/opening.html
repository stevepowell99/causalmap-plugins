The analysis follows the usual discipline of qualitative evaluation: the question agreed before the evidence is read, a codebook fixed before anything is counted, and an audit trail another analyst could follow. Where the question calls for a recognised method, such as contribution analysis, process tracing or your own rubric, it follows that method's published practice.

  1. Settle the question with you, and what the answer will feed.
  2. Code the passages that bear on it, against definitions written down first.
  3. Count by code. Every number is recomputed by a program from the coded passages, and every quotation is matched word for word against its document.
  4. Read it again. A second coder recodes the documents without seeing the first coding, and a fresh reader checks every claim and quotation against them.
  5. Report in which every figure and quotation opens its evidence, with a Word copy and the whole run in one file.

What it claims. Counts of what respondents said, such as "9 of 14 described a change in sales". Not estimates of effect, and no wider than the sample allows.

Your documents go to Anthropic under your own Claude plan, and nowhere else. For client data, switch off "Help improve Claude" in Settings › Privacy.
